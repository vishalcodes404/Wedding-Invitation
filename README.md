# Sunil Kumar & Santhiya - Wedding Invitation

Open this folder in Antigravity (File > Open Folder), open index.html and preview in a browser.

- index.html - content and drawn illustrations (names, dates, venues, quotes)
- style.css - colours (top of file), fonts, layout
- script.js - tap-to-open intro, golden gates, countdown, RSVP number, music
- assets/song.mp3 - ADD YOUR SONG HERE (plays on open; music button toggles it)

Quick edits: countdown time and WhatsApp number are in script.js.
